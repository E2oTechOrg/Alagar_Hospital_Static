The content on this website is shared to help you understand our services. It is not medical advice. Please read this disclaimer carefully.

1. In an emergency

If you or someone else has a medical emergency, such as a serious injury, a suspected fracture after an accident, heavy bleeding, chest pain, breathing trouble or loss of consciousness, call 108 or come to the hospital straight away. Do not wait for a reply to a website form. Hospital line: +91 88072 62725.

2. General information only

The pages on trauma, spine, paediatric ortho, arthroscopy, arthroplasty, general medicine, women and child care and other topics give general information. They do not describe every condition, treatment option or risk.

3. Not a substitute for professional advice

Information on this website does not replace a consultation with a qualified doctor. Always seek the advice of your doctor about your own condition. Never ignore, delay or stop medical advice because of something you read here.

4. No diagnosis or treatment online

We do not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment through the website, forms, email or social media. Treatment decisions are made only after a proper examination and, where needed, tests.

5. Treatment results vary

Every patient is different. Medical and surgical treatment, including joint replacement, arthroscopy and spine surgery, carries risks, and results depend on your condition, health, age and how well you follow advice. Nothing on this website promises or guarantees a particular outcome. Your doctor will explain the benefits, risks and alternatives before any procedure.

6. Doctors and availability

Doctor names, qualifications and specialities are shown for information. Availability, timings and services can change. Please call the hospital to confirm before you visit.

7. Third-party content

Links, maps and social media pages on this website belong to other companies. We do not control or endorse their content.

8. Your responsibility

  • give your doctor complete and honest information about your health, allergies and medicines;
  • follow the advice, medicines and follow-up plan you are given;
  • contact the hospital promptly if your condition worsens.
