Good care is a partnership between patients, families and the hospital team. This page sets out what you can expect from Alagar Hospital and what we ask of you in return.

1. Your rights as a patient

As a patient you have the right to:

  • be treated with respect, dignity and compassion, regardless of your age, gender, religion, caste, language or background;
  • receive prompt attention in an emergency;
  • know the name and role of the doctors and staff caring for you;
  • be told about your condition, the proposed treatment, its benefits, risks and alternatives, in a language you understand;
  • give or refuse consent before any procedure or surgery, and be told what may happen if you refuse;
  • ask for a second opinion;
  • privacy and confidentiality of your medical information, which is shared only as needed for your care or as required by law;
  • ask for a copy of your records, reports and discharge summary, subject to applicable law;
  • know the expected charges and receive a bill for services provided;
  • give feedback or make a complaint without fear of it affecting your care.

2. Your responsibilities

To help us care for you safely, we ask you to:

  • give accurate and complete information about your health, past illnesses, allergies and medicines;
  • follow the treatment plan, take medicines as advised and attend follow-up visits;
  • tell your doctor or nurse if you do not understand something or if your condition changes;
  • treat doctors, staff and other patients with courtesy;
  • pay hospital charges on time as explained at registration and discharge;
  • look after hospital property.

3. Visitors and attenders

To protect patients and keep wards calm, visitors are asked to follow the hospital's visiting hours and instructions from staff. Too many visitors can disturb recovery and increase the risk of infection. Smoking, alcohol, tobacco and other prohibited items are not allowed on the hospital premises.

4. Respectful environment

We do not accept abuse, threats or violence against our doctors, nurses or staff. The hospital may take appropriate action, including asking a person to leave the premises or informing the authorities, in such cases.

5. Feedback and complaints

We welcome your feedback. If you have a concern about your care, billing or staff behaviour, please speak to the staff at the reception or write to the hospital administration. Concerns can also be raised with the Managing Director.

We will listen, look into it, and reply as soon as we reasonably can.
