This policy explains how to book and change appointments, and how billing and payments are handled at Alagar Hospital. Hospital staff will guide you at the reception for specific details.

1. Booking an appointment

You can request an appointment by phone, by visiting the hospital, or through the form on our website.

  • A website request is only a request. It is confirmed when our staff contact you or confirm it at the hospital.
  • Please give a working phone number so that we can reach you.
  • Walk-in patients are seen subject to doctor availability.

2. Hospital timings

  • Emergency and in-patient care: open 24/7
  • Out-patient (OP), Monday to Saturday: 10 AM - 2 PM and 5 PM - 9 PM

Timings may change on holidays or when a doctor is unavailable. Please call to confirm.

3. What to bring

  • a photo ID and any previous hospital registration details;
  • earlier prescriptions, reports, X-rays, MRI or CT films and discharge summaries;
  • a list of medicines you take and any known allergies;
  • a family member or attender, if you are old, a child, or unwell.

4. Rescheduling and cancellation

If you cannot attend, please call us as early as possible so that the time can be given to another patient and your visit can be rescheduled. If you arrive late, you may be seen after other scheduled patients, depending on the doctor's availability.

5. When the doctor is unavailable

Doctors may be called away for emergencies or surgeries. When this happens we will try to inform you and offer another time or doctor. We appreciate your patience.

6. Fees and billing

  • Consultation, investigation, procedure, room and pharmacy charges follow the hospital's current fee schedule.
  • You will be told the charges, or an estimate, at registration or before a procedure or admission.
  • Estimates can change if your condition or treatment plan changes. We will explain any major change.
  • A bill is given for all payments made.

7. Payment

Payment is made at the billing counter. Please enquire at the reception about the payment modes accepted. For admissions and surgeries, an advance deposit may be requested. Final settlement is done at discharge.

8. Refunds

If you have paid for a service that was not provided, please contact the hospital administration. Such requests are reviewed case by case, and eligible amounts are refunded through a suitable payment mode. Charges for services already provided are not refundable.

9. Insurance and government schemes

If you plan to use health insurance or a government scheme, please ask at the reception before admission about whether it can be used and what documents are needed. Amounts not covered by your insurer or scheme are payable by the patient.

10. Admission and discharge

Admission is advised by the treating doctor. Please follow the hospital's instructions on visiting hours and attenders. At discharge you will receive a discharge summary, medicines and follow-up advice.
