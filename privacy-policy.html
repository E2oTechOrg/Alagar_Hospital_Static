Alagar Hospital respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you use this website we may collect:

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address and the message you type in the enquiry or appointment form;
  • Technical details: basic information such as browser type, device and pages visited, which your browser sends automatically.

Please do not put detailed medical history or reports in the website form. Share these with our doctors in person.

2. How we use your information

  • to reply to your enquiry and to arrange or confirm appointments;
  • to contact you about your request by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email;
  • to improve the website and our services;
  • to meet legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Patient records at the hospital

Information you give during registration, consultation, tests and treatment at the hospital forms your medical record. We keep it confidential and handle it as required by medical ethics and applicable law. This policy covers the website; records at the hospital are handled under the hospital's own confidentiality practices.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with staff and doctors who need it to help you;
  • with service providers who support our website, communication or hospital systems, and who are expected to keep it secure;
  • with your consent;
  • when required by law, a court or a government authority, or to protect life and safety.

5. Cookies and third-party services

This website loads fonts, icons and an embedded map from third parties (Google Fonts, Font Awesome and Google Maps). When these load, those providers may receive your IP address and browser details and may set cookies. Please read their privacy policies for details. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No website or internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and for our records, or as required by law. Medical records are kept for the period required by applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it, where we are not required to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent to being contacted.

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults. If a child needs care, a parent or legal guardian should contact us and give information on the child's behalf.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
